Four steps to a
modded game.

Most of the time goes into the downloads. You can walk away while it runs.

  1. Download and open it

    Get KOTOR-Mod-Installer.exe from the latest release and run it. That one file is the whole app.

  2. Point it at your game

    In Settings, add your KOTOR 1 and KOTOR 2 folders and sign in to DeadlyStream. For builds with Nexus Mods files, add your Nexus API key too. Free Nexus accounts click one download button per mod; Premium is fully automatic.

  3. Pick a build and press install

    Open Mod Builds, choose a build such as KOTOR 1 Full Build, then Download & Install All. Mods go in one after another in the recommended order, with live progress.

  4. Tweak it later

    Use the Library to switch mods on or off, or add any other mod archive you have. Conflicts warns you when two mods touch the same files, or when a mod's readme says it doesn't get along with another.

The app downloads mods through your own DeadlyStream and Nexus Mods accounts and runs the installers that come with each mod. It includes no mod content itself. TSLPatcher and HoloPatcher belong to their authors.
